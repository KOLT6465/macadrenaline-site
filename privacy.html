Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 13, 2026. Last updated: July 31, 2026.

Adrenaline: Game Boost processes selected screen content locally on your Mac.

Data Collection

Adrenaline does not collect personal data, does not track users, does not show ads, and does not use analytics.

Screen Capture

Adrenaline uses macOS ScreenCaptureKit and macOS Screen Recording permission so you can choose a window or display to capture. Captured frames are processed locally on your Mac for frame generation, upscaling, and overlay presentation.

No Uploads

Adrenaline does not upload captured screen content, game footage, images, video, or performance data to any server.

Third-Party Services

Adrenaline does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, tracking SDKs, or cloud processing services.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact koltin@heapsea.com.
