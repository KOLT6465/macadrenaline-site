Before You Contact Support

  • Make sure Screen Recording permission is enabled for Adrenaline in macOS System Settings.
  • Try capturing a window instead of a full display if overlay alignment looks wrong.
  • Use lower frame generation quality if your Mac misses live frame timing.

Privacy

Adrenaline processes selected capture content locally on your Mac and does not upload captured content.
